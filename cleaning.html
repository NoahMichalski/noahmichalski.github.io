Mileage Reports
Cancelled jobs remain in the mileage route because the crew may still have driven there. Mileage uses routed driving distance only; unavailable routes are marked missing rather than replaced with straight-line mileage.
Employee Driver List
Every unique employee found in your saved days and analyzed schedules appears here. Fix misspelled names, choose the normal mileage status, and optionally set the date that driver status began. Leave the date blank if it applies to all saved history.
Daily Driver Overrides
Overrides are saved by date and crew. Use these when someone drove a separate vehicle, trained, switched vehicles, or did not drive that day.
Mileage exclusions
An excluded person or crew is omitted only from mileage reports. They remain in efficiency and weekly operations reports.
